Privacy

The extension talks to Snapp Market and Digikala Jet, and to nothing else. There is no server of ours, no analytics, no telemetry and no third-party script.

What is stored, and where

All of it in chrome.storage.local, on your machine. Uninstalling deletes it.

Delivery point and label Every price and delivery fee depends on where you are
Filters and sort mode So the popup opens the way you left it
Last search and its results So reopening the popup does not re-run a 45-request search
Recent queries Search box history, capped at eight
Snapp Market session token Sent to svc.snapp.market so prices reflect your account
Anonymous token and device id Used when you are not signed in
Saved Snapp Market addresses So you can pick one instead of typing coordinates

What is sent, and to whom

svc.snapp.market Your coordinates, the search term, and your bearer token
api.digikalajet.ir Your coordinates and the search term. No token — Jet's search needs none

These are the same requests the two websites make from your browser when you use them normally. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

The session token

If you have snapp.market open and signed in, a content script reads the bearer token the site already keeps in its own localStorage and hands it to the extension. That is what makes Pro delivery fees and personalised campaign prices show up. The token goes to svc.snapp.market and nowhere else, and it is never logged.

Without a signed-in tab the extension mints its own anonymous token through the public grant the website uses, tied to a random identifier it generates rather than to any account.

Permissions

storage for your settings, tabs to open the store you picked, and host access to the four Snapp Market and Digikala Jet origins — which is also what lets the two content scripts read your session and delivery point out of pages you already have open. Nothing else.

The full policy, including the developer tooling that captures traffic locally, is in docs/PRIVACY.md.
