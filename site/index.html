How it works

Snapp Market's own nearby-stores endpoint previews only ten campaign items per store. That is nowhere near the shelf, so the extension goes and reads each one.

  1. Takes your delivery point. Automatically from a signed-in snapp.market tab, or entered as coordinates.
  2. Lists every store that delivers there and is running the orange-discount campaign — around 45 in central Tehran.
  3. Reads each store's full campaign shelf, six requests at a time. This is the step that makes the results complete.
  4. Matches your product by name or by product code, with Persian text handling that knows مینو inside دومینو is not a match.
  5. Ranks and links. Every result has a button that opens that store.

Why that store is first

The default ordering applies four rules, after pushing closed stores and out-of-stock items to the bottom.

Rule Why
1 Discount percent, in 5% steps The headline reason to use it — bucketed, so 41% does not beat 40%
2 Snapp Market Pro stores first Pro charges a fraction of the normal delivery fee
3 Cheaper delivery Among equals, the cheaper trip wins
4 Cheaper final price Last tiebreak

Two other modes are one click away: cheapest total (item plus delivery) and lowest delivery fee. Every card shows the total with delivery and the store's minimum basket, so a 500-Toman ice cream cannot quietly mislead you.

What you get

The whole shelf

Each store's full تخفیف نارنجی catalogue, not the ten-item preview the listing endpoint hands out.

Your prices

With a signed-in tab open it uses your own session, so Pro delivery fees and personalised campaign prices are the ones you see.

Name or product code

Type a name with suggestions from Snapp Market's own autocomplete, or paste a product id to compare one exact SKU across stores.

Persian text that works

Arabic and Persian letter forms folded, digits normalised, word-aware matching. Searching پفک مینو does not return Domino ice cream.

Digikala Jet too

A second source behind a checkbox, with Rial prices converted to Toman so the two platforms are comparable.

Nothing phones home

Requests go to Snapp Market and Digikala Jet and nowhere else. No analytics, no server of ours, no runtime dependencies.

Install

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Arc (Chromium 111+). There is no compile step — the extension is plain ES modules the browser loads directly. 

git clone https://github.com/amiranmanesh/discount-hunter-extension.git
cd discount-hunter-extension

Then open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the extension/ folder.

Open snapp.market in a tab and sign in before your first search. The extension picks up your saved addresses from there, and your session is what unlocks Pro delivery fees. It works signed out too, with anonymous pricing.
